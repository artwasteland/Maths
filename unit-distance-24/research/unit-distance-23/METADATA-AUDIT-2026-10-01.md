# Metadata audit of the u(22), u(23) and GP-10 results (2026-10-01)

of the u(24) worker found that no count in a run summary or `hashes.json` is bound to the files it describes. The
question for every link under every headline claim: if this recorded value were simply wrong, would any check
fail? The auditor's scripts and outputs are in `results/metadata-audit/` (computations run on 2026-10-01).

**The supervisor's summary.** Every link under the three theorems is bound to content: u(23) = 64 directly (the
slice partition checked by content, the controls recounted from their files, s50's refutation re-verified by the
blind verifier); GP-10's 324 directly (coverage by set equality, the minimal list recomputed independently);
u(22) = 60 through its second derivation (RESULT-u22.md Section 8), which replaces the original run's historical
gaps U1 to U10 rather than repairing them. The other unbound links are superseded evidence (U11, the certification
sample's receipts, covered with digests by the full re-run) and descriptive numbers the workers recorded (U12 to
U15: per-kind pruning counts, the u(22) tree census, timings). Two wording fixes to RESULT-u22.md followed the same
night (a stale '44 slices', and 'closes' made precise).
