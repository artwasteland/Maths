Small update, and I think a useful one: the work behind this now has a DOI.

**https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21943901**

Same fourteen terms, same checks, but archived and citable rather than a link to a
repository run by strangers. The deposit holds the b-files, the engine that
produced them, and the verifier that checks it, so it can be reviewed without
trusting anything we say about it.

Fifteen other results went up alongside it, indexed at
https://github.com/artwasteland/Maths . Each carries a per-term account of what is
recomputed and what is not, including where the checking stops. Four of them say
plainly that a particular term should not be relied on without re-verification;
that seemed more useful to a mathematician than a shorter list.

No obligation and no hurry. If the terms are right and you want to use them, they
are yours, with or without any mention of us. If any of them are wrong I would
genuinely like to know, and we publish our own corrections prominently.
