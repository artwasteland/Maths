%N Number of noncappable change-ringing sequences of length n for 7 bells.
%C A change-ringing sequence of length k for 7 bells is a sequence of k distinct rows (permutations of 1..7) beginning at rounds (1,2,...,7) in which every row differs from the previous one by a "change": each bell stays put or moves one position, i.e. the rows differ by a product of disjoint adjacent transpositions (see J. K. Sonsteby's definition, the source of A324942-A324949). Such a sequence is "cyclic" if its last row can return to rounds in one further change (A324948), and "path" if no closure is required (A324949). It is NONCAPPABLE when its last row is NOT one change away from rounds, so the sequence cannot be capped to close into a cyclic touch. Equivalently a(n) = (path sequences of length n) - (cyclic sequences of length n) = A324949(n) - A324948(n).
%C In graph terms: rows are the 7! vertices of the change-ringing graph on 7 bells (a Cayley graph of the symmetric group S_7 generated by the nonempty products of disjoint adjacent transpositions; its degree is A000071(7+1) = Fibonacci(7+1)-1). a(L) counts simple paths of L vertices that start at the identity and END at a vertex that is NOT adjacent to the identity.
%C a(1) = a(2) = 0: a single row, or rounds plus any one neighbour, is always cappable. Leading zeros are kept so that a(L) is indexed by sequence length L, matching the sibling sequences A324948 and A324949.

%e For 7 bells the shortest noncappable sequence has length 3: rounds, then two changes ending on a row that cannot reach rounds in one change; a(3) = 224.
