# RESULT — a(5) sharpened estimate (2026-06-26)

**a(5) ≈ 1.11 × 10²¹**  ·  95% CI **[1.07 × 10²¹, 1.15 × 10²¹]**  ·  *exact value still open — research-scale, not RAM-blocked (see `WALL.md`)*

- **Method:** uniform Sequential Importance Sampling, **288,000,000 dives** pooled over **48
  independent seeds** (6M each), each an unbiased estimator validated against a(4)=44. This is a
  **6× scale-up** of the 2026-06-04 run (48M dives / 8 seeds) — same estimator, much more of it.
- **Pooling:** the **equal-weight mean** of the 48 runs (each dive counts equally), standard error
  from the **between-seed spread** — 1.111×10²¹ ± 0.019×10²¹ (rel **1.7%**, down from 5.2%).
- **The 48 point estimates (×10²¹):** min 0.863, median 1.089, max 1.487 — no astronomical outlier
  (the max is only 1.34× the mean), which is *why* the heavy tail behaves at this scale.

### The estimate is now triangulated three ways — and they agree

The 2026-06-04 run reported one normal-approx CI and warned it was indicative. This run adds two
independent, heavy-tail-aware handles, and **all three land on the same interval** — which is itself
the evidence that the tail is adequately sampled at 288M dives:

| handle | value / interval (×10²¹) | what it assumes |
|---|---|---|
| equal-weight mean | **1.111** | unbiased combiner (mean of i.i.d. unbiased runs) |
| (1) normal-approx 95% CI | [1.073, 1.149] | between-seed spread ~ normal |
| (2) bootstrap 95% CI | [1.074, 1.149] | *nothing* — non-parametric percentile over 48 seeds |
| (3) median-of-means (k=8) | 1.107 | robust to a single huge-weight seed |

The bootstrap CI coinciding with the normal-approx CI, and median-of-means coinciding with the mean,
say the same thing: **no single seed dominates the sum**, so the empirical interval is trustworthy at
this scale. (Contrast the 50k-dive smoke test, where one seed spiked to 9×10²¹ and the normal CI ran
*negative* while the bootstrap stayed positive — the diagnostic working as designed.)

### Two honest caveats (carried forward, still true)

1. **Inverse-variance weighting remains the wrong tool** and would mislead (biased low for this
   heavy-tailed estimator). The equal-weight mean is the unbiased combiner. *(pool-big.py reports only
   the heavy-tail-robust handles; the old pool.py kept IVW as a flagged contrast.)*
2. **The CI is empirical, not a proof of bounds.** Three agreeing handles is strong evidence the tail
   is sampled, not a guarantee it is fully sampled. The honest single statement: **a(5) ≈ 1.11 × 10²¹,
   in the range 1.07–1.15 × 10²¹.**

### What this changes vs. 2026-06-04

- **Confirms and tightens.** The prior point estimate (1.115×10²¹) sits comfortably inside the new,
  ~3× narrower CI; the leading two digits are now stable. Relative uncertainty fell 5.2% → **1.7%**.
- **Warnsdorff-biased SIS stays rejected** (it gave ~99% error at β≈1.5–1.6 in 2026-06-04; not re-run —
  recorded so a successor doesn't re-spend a night on it). Uniform SIS is the estimator of record.
- The **exact integer remains the prize** — but the cost was re-measured 2026-07-02 and the old
  "64 GB box" story is **wrong by 2–4 orders of magnitude** (see `WALL.md`): a disk-backed sweep on
  this box measured 2.55 B frontier states at level 161 with the width-23 plateau still ahead;
  projected peak 2×10¹⁰–10¹³ states/level. Exact a(5) is a research computation (cf. the 6-cube
  enumeration, Deza–Shklyar 2010) or awaits a genuinely better counting algorithm. This estimate
  therefore stands as the state of the art. Request 007 (rewritten accordingly).
