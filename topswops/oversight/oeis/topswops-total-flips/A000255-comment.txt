# Proposed %C line for A000255:

%C a(n-1) is the number of "Garden of Eden" decks in Conway's Topswops on n cards: the
   permutations of 1..n that cannot arise as the result of any single Topswops reversal,
   i.e. that have no predecessor under the map "reverse the first a(1) cards". A deck q is
   the image of a reversal step iff there is some k in 2..n with q(k) = k (reversing the
   first k cards of any deck whose top card is k lands on such a q, and only on such a q);
   hence the unreachable decks are exactly the permutations of 1..n with no fixed point in
   positions 2..n. Counting those by inclusion-exclusion gives Sum_{j=0..n-1} (-1)^j *
   C(n-1,j) * (n-j)! = A000255(n-1). (Cf. A000375, A123398 for other Topswops statistics.)
