GP-11 consultation conclusion, 2026-10-01

A six-day finish is a plausible aggressive target, not a defensible promise from this pilot. Compute is affordable; the certificate residue is the critical uncertainty. The same measured policy leaves 13/500 unresolved, projecting to about 11666 graphs before full-population propagation. A nominal Wilson 95% interval is 6846..19730. This is a range for the bounded per-graph automated policy, not the eventual number of analyst jobs. The policy was developed during this exploratory pilot; hardware/budget and model uncertainty exceed sampling uncertainty alone.

The pilot did not run an all-B11 classification, new B11 profile, independent G0 recount/witness replay or G5 review. The 500-graph combined sample plus two old proof dependencies was accepted by the actual frozen GP10 checker with 479 embedded, 8 refuted and 13 unknown. Its ACCEPT is explicitly conditional. The full-population TU count of 3323 is a proposer census, not 3323 newly independently replayed certificates. The full-poset propagation counts are structural measurements, not a checked 49585-record classification. No repository files were changed; final git status matches the initial status, including the owner's existing changes.

COST MODEL
One ideal four-core machine-hour means 14400 single-core seconds of useful work. Search and CAS child workloads here are single-threaded. The prototype measured per-graph times, including individual certificate checks where performed. Reusing those timings in the schedule TU -> lattice18 -> degree2 extension -> host -> tree -> null -> real -> framed/pinned msolve -> all-pairs portfolio costs 438.657 seconds per 500 graphs, or 27.34 ideal four-core machine-hours over 448708. This is an inferred ordered-pass cost, not an end-to-end measured B11 runtime; separate method timing and cache/CPU effects matter. Starting extension before lattice costs 29.22 ideal machine-hours in the same model. Numeric reconstruction and lattice36/30 added no unique decisions in this pilot and are not part of that schedule.

Budget 40..100 four-core machine-hours for this bounded pass and engineering/verification/assembly overhead, assuming roughly 50..80% useful producer utilization and several hours for serial checking. This is a planning range, not a confidence interval. At 25 available four-core workers, producer capacity is not the six-day bottleneck. A further 120 single-core seconds on 7000..20000 remaining graphs costs another 58..167 ideal machine-hours. After applying the same50..80% utilization allowance, about110..450 machine-hours is a reasonable budget for the first pass plus one substantial escalation, with no asserted reduction in UNKNOWN. The larger budget is a cost commitment, not evidence of completeness.

The existing checker processed an all-unknown sample-profile B11 run in 110.25 seconds at 85.2 MiB main-process peak RSS; this measures input/record/coverage plumbing only. It excludes exact proofs, controls and minimality. The accepted GP10 full profile took 3156 seconds in its recorded environment; multiplying by 13.53 gives about 11.9 serial hours as a rough scheduling reference, not a measured GP11 forecast. Reserve 12..24 hours for final replay/minimality, late certificate repairs, review closure and result generation. Start partial/full-size rehearsals early so this reserve is replaced by actual timings.

RESIDUE AND THE IMPORTANT OBJECTION
Uniform random graphs are not uniform proof work. In the sample, minimum-degree-three graphs were 17/500: 5 embedded, 7 refuted, 5 unknown. Degree-two graphs were 483/500: 474 embedded, 1 refuted, 8 unknown. The population has 10915 min-degree-three graphs and 437793 degree-two graphs. A follow-up pilot must oversample the former and the unresolved frontier, with weights reported.
The measured 479 positive seeds cover 49557 distinct B11 vertices in the full poset. The eight negative seeds cover only 28. Mean individual positive coverage is 394.3 (median186, maximum5081); negative coverage is 3.625 (median1.5, maximum14), including the seed. Overlaps are why these means cannot be multiplied into a total saving. The order's maximal frontier has 24037 graphs, but newly exposed lower layers must also be scheduled after failed/refuted maxima. It is not a permanent cap on work.
Ordinary subgraph propagation cannot make one new minimal forbidden graph prove a different new minimal forbidden graph. That makes the new minimal-forbidden antichain fundamentally different from the easy embedding mass. TU equivalence classes and reusable checked geometric templates can reduce the work; the eight negative sample seeds suggest that ordinary upward propagation alone will not. GP10's overall propagated percentage cannot be used as an analyst discount.
More time also does not fix a completed no-move/no-target search. Track that outcome separately from timeout and memory exhaustion. The 13 pilot survivors resisted the tested default frame/saturation searches and 10-second all-pairs portfolio, but this is not evidence that they are forbidden. Two of the preceding fifteen were embedded by a small order-11 msolve port, illustrating why numerical/search failures must stay UNKNOWN.

THROUGHPUT GATE
Let F be the post-propagation, post-TU number of independent frontier/family work units, q the measured accepted units per analyst-hour, C the effective simultaneous analyst capacity after queues/returns, and H the hours before the final-check reserve. Need F <= 0.7*q*C*H to leave a 30% margin, plus a credible plan for the hard tail. For example 2000 independent units at one accepted unit/hour on 50 effective concurrent tasks require 40 hours of analysis, about57 with the margin. Ten thousand units at that rate require200 hours before reserve. The user's large credit balance can fund attempts, but neither queue capacity nor proof-discovery throughput follows from the balance. Decide deployment levels from the early pilot instead of assuming them.

Return the actual small JSON certificate and dependency closure, checker receipt bound to their bytes, and a short mathematical explanation. Coordinates alone go to a separately tracked exactification queue. Scripts/reproduction commands are useful additions, not a replacement for a retained certificate. Confirm a real diff-only Codex task can return the artifact before launching a large wave. Use content-addressed per-graph directories and leased task IDs. Keep uncompleted cases durable even when a session times out. Fetch each Claude branch to a distinct ref; do not use shared FETCH_HEAD as an identity. OTHER RISKS
G0's 24-edge cap and K4-free generator restriction must be part of a proved, recorded enumeration domain. Independent witnesses need coverage, not merely valid individual maps. The consulted B11 enumeration is not that replay.
A G-v embedding is one chosen configuration, not all realizations; its neighbours may be too far apart or both new intersections occupied. A failure cannot refute G. Six sampled degree-two graphs had no B10-table base under the eligible deletions: block gluing or another search is necessary. Arbitrary stored number fields need actual generator conversion; the prototype found this, repaired it and rechecked all positives.
Full JSON posets and certificate object graphs can defeat 'lean' memory claims. Keep certificate bytes and relation storage out of Python object heaps. Global process accounting must include CAS children and BLAS threads.
A B11 wrapper has new trusted classification/control logic even if certificate arithmetic is unchanged. Arrange the outside review at project start. Test every negative fixture at its intended rule, not an earlier incidental schema/hash failure.
Changing u(24)'s filter mid-run to consume a growing GP11 list would require its own version-bound provenance and checks. Keep the two projects' critical paths independent unless that integration is already authorized and reviewed.

Integration note from scratch measurements: embed and refute both have a module named round2.py. Importing all source directories into one undifferentiated sys.path can select the wrong module. Prefer isolated entry points or explicit namespaced loading; test mixed-stage deployment. Degree-two PARI extension must remap arbitrary stored field generators, including non-monic input polynomials, before applying nfroots/rnfequation. Merely copying coordinate coefficients into a newly reduced number field is wrong; final E-exact checks catch it.

TU quotient opportunity: for a graph with no forbidden endpoint, adding all six-gadget forced edges preserves embeddability in both directions. Closed F398-free graphs are among the 294516 no-TU-hit B11 members. Thus one may search closed representatives and lift positive results by E-super and negative ones by a nonempty R-tu chain to a checked closed representative. Ordinary subgraph propagation cannot carry a refutation from a supergraph back to its subgraph. TU reversal plus ordinary R-sub pointers can create cycles; require direct seed proof and acyclic dependencies, not mutual assertions around a quotient class.
