Generated by `tools/write_independent_checks.py` from the JSON files in `runs/independent-checks-2026-09-25/` and
`runs/G4-second-certificates-2026-09-25/`. Every number below is read from those files.

`RESULT.md` rests on one checker (`check/gp10check.py` with its kernel): every certificate, every count and the

- **Input.** 33,162 graphs, 33,162 distinct isomorphism classes (nauty certificates); the

- **Minimality, recounted from scratch.** Every one-edge deletion of every refuted graph was rebuilt and looked up

  refuted graphs are not minimal. Biconnected deletions missing from B10: 0.
- **Every exact embedding, re-evaluated.** 3,879 embeddings: the root of each minimal polynomial found by
  bisection at 120 digits, every coordinate evaluated there. Worst edge-length residue 6.16e-99;
  at 240 digits it falls to 6.72e-219, so the residue is rounding and the edges are exactly unit.
  Smallest distance between two points of any embedding: 0.00240617.
- **Every pointer.** 28,774 subgraph-of-an-embedded-graph pointers and 185
  contains-a-refuted-graph pointers: the map is injective and edge-preserving in the contract's direction, the
  target carries the required verdict, and every chain ends at a non-pointer certificate. Broken chains:
  0.
- **Cross-consistency.** The graphs of B10 obtained from an L10 member by adding edges number 509;
  the refuted graphs number 509; the two sets are equal. So no
  embedded graph contains a forbidden one, and every refuted graph contains a minimal one.
- **The same, by a method with no canonical forms.** VF2 subgraph monomorphism, the 100 densest
  embedded graphs (18 to 20 edges, of 32,653) against every L10 member:
  10,595 pairs, 0 containments. Capped at 100 hosts (about 15 s each).

## 3. Trying to embed the 324 anyway (`tools/embed_redteam.py`)

If a refutation certificate were wrong, its graph would have an embedding, and a hard enough numerical search
might find it. This one uses numpy only: one angle per spanning-tree edge (tree edges are unit by construction),
batched Levenberg-Marquardt from 16,384 random starts per graph, and a candidate counts only if refinement at
50 digits drives every edge residue below 1e-35 while no two points come closer than 1e-6. A search that finds
nothing proves nothing unless its power is measured, so the same rule, with the same budget as a ceiling, was spent on two
embeddable controls per member: the member minus one edge (embeddable by minimality), and an embedded B10 graph
with the same number of edges.

- **Members:** 324 searched, **0 embeddings found**.
- **Near-misses:** 1,182 float candidates on 52 members passed the fast filter, and none
  survived refinement. Of the 152 whose refined gap was recorded (up to three per member), the largest
  remaining distance between two points was 3.8e-11: each slid into a collision, which is how these
  graphs fail to embed.
- **Controls:** member minus an edge, 324 of 324 found; look-alike with the same edge count, 324
  of 324 found (319 of them with the same degree sequence as their member).

| edges | members | members embedded | minus-edge controls found | look-alike controls found |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | 4 | 0 | 4 of 4 | 4 of 4 |
| 16 | 126 | 0 | 126 of 126 | 126 of 126 |
| 17 | 191 | 0 | 191 of 191 | 191 of 191 |
| 18 | 3 | 0 | 3 of 3 | 3 of 3 |

This is evidence, not proof: it could find a counterexample to a certificate, and on graphs that do embed it finds
one at the rate in the table.

## 6. Every polynomial identity, without FLINT (`tools/modp_identities.py`)

Both verifiers expand the polynomial identities in python-flint. This check shares no code with FLINT, sympy or
either verifier: it regenerates each system from the graph6 string and evaluates both sides of every identity at
3 random points modulo each of 2^61-1 and 2^127-1, in Python integers. A false identity of degree d
survives a random point with probability at most d/p, so none can pass unnoticed here in practice.

- **The 324:** real-dag 97, null-dag 65, real 24, null 22 certificates, 2,071 identities per point (every cofactor sum, every derivation
  row, every sum-of-squares decomposition): 0 failures.
- **The 74 GP certificates:** 7 polynomial ones, 24 identities per point: 0 failures.
- **Red controls:** 8 of 8 mutations (a coefficient changed, a
  term dropped, a row emptied, an SOS weight or constant changed, a frame on a non-edge) failed as required.
- Not an identity, so not covered here: a Sturm certificate's claim that its univariate f has no real root. That
  count is made by the official checker, by the blind verifier, and by sympy's `count_roots` inside the blind one.

## What these checks do not cover

- The refutations are checked by two implementations from different model families, built from the same written
  lemmas (`PROOFS.md`, the u(22) gadget proofs, AMP Section 4): a mistake in a lemma itself would pass both.
  Both do their exact polynomial arithmetic in python-flint; every identity was also re-checked without it
  (section 6).
- B10's completeness was established at gate G0 (geng's biconnected count matches OEIS A002218, and an independent
  C matcher reproduced the F9 filter byte for byte); nothing here re-derives it.
- The audit's pynauty and the pipeline's canonical labelling both come from nauty.
